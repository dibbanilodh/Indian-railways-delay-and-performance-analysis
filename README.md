# Indian Railways Delay & Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Indian Railways Delay & Performance Analysis** project is an interactive Tableau dashboard created to analyze train punctuality, delays, and station-wise performance of Indian Railways.
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of railway performance and helps identify punctual trains, highly delayed trains, delay patterns, and stations with significant delays.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze train delay and punctuality performance.
- Identify the most punctual trains.
- Identify the most delayed trains.
- Analyze the distribution of train delays.
- Compare delays across different railway stations.
- Categorize train-station delays based on severity.
- Present railway performance through interactive visualizations.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes KPI cards for:
- Total Stations
- Total Trains
- Average Delay
- Maximum Delay
- Minimum Delay
These KPIs provide a quick overview of the current dataset and update dynamically based on the underlying data and dashboard filters.

### 2. Top 10 Most Punctual Trains

A bar chart displays the top 10 trains with the lowest average delay, helping identify the best-performing and most punctual services.

### 3. Delay Distribution by Train

The dashboard categorizes delay records into different time ranges to show how frequently trains experience short, moderate, or severe delays.
This helps understand the overall distribution and severity of train delays.

### 4. Train-Station Delay Category

A pie chart categorizes train-station delay records into:
- Low Delay
- Medium Delay
- High Delay
This provides an easy way to understand the proportion of delays by severity.

### 5. Delay by Stations

A station-wise delay visualization shows the top 7 railway stations with maximum average delays.
It helps identify stations with relatively higher average delays and can highlight areas that may require further operational analysis.

### 6. Top 10 Most Delayed Trains

A visualization highlights the top 10 trains with the highest average delays.
This helps identify services that experience significant delays and may require further investigation.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to:
- Monitor overall railway punctuality.

- Identify trains with significant delays.
